# Hot Tub Tri Machine Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 17 August 2026

## 1\. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Hot Tub Tri Machine ("we", "us", or "our") handles personal information when you use the Hot Tub Tri Machine website at https://hottubtrimachine.au/.

Hot Tub Tri Machine is a project operated by David Cass under the DC Digital brand.

## 2\. Information we collect

The Hot Tub Tri Machine website is primarily an informational and podcast website.

If you use the contact form, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

The website may also receive limited technical information as part of normal website operation, hosting, security, and troubleshooting.

## 3\. How we use information

Information submitted through the contact form is used to:

* receive and respond to your enquiry;
* communicate with you about your message; and
* maintain the security and operation of the website.

We do not use contact-form information for unrelated marketing purposes unless we have an appropriate basis to do so.

We do not sell personal information.

## 4\. Contact form processing

Information submitted through the contact form is transmitted to us by email.

Hot Tub Tri Machine does **not** maintain a database of contact-form submissions.

Once a message has been transmitted by email, it may remain in the email systems and mailboxes used to receive and manage the enquiry and may be retained in accordance with those systems' normal retention practices.

## 5\. Third-party services

The website uses third-party cloud and email services to provide website hosting, contact-form processing, email delivery, and related technical functionality.

These providers may process information on our behalf and may have their own privacy policies.

## 6\. Disclosure of information

We may disclose personal information:

* to service providers necessary to operate the website and contact form;
* where required or authorised by law; or
* where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of DC Digital, Hot Tub Tri Machine, or others.

We do not sell personal information.

## 7\. Cookies and analytics

The website may use technologies that are necessary for normal website operation, security, or functionality.

Where analytics or other optional tracking technologies are used, their use will be subject to the relevant provider's practices and applicable privacy requirements.

## 8\. Data retention

Hot Tub Tri Machine does not retain contact-form submissions in an application database.

Information contained in emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations.

## 9\. Access and correction

You may contact us to request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

**Email:** contact@dcdigital.au

## 10\. International disclosure

Some technology and service providers used to operate the website, process contact-form submissions, or deliver email may process information outside Australia.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to address the handling of personal information by those providers in accordance with applicable Australian privacy requirements.

## 11\. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
